GET TO KNOW US

About Our Church

Discover who we are, what we believe, and why we exist.

A Church Built on Faith, Hope and Love

Isai Aradhana Church is a welcoming Christian community committed to sharing God's love, strengthening faith, and serving people.

Our church brings people together through worship, prayer, fellowship, Bible study, and community activities.

Sharing God's Word
Building Strong Faith
Serving Our Community
Learn More

Our Mission

To share the love of Christ, encourage spiritual growth, and serve our community with compassion.

Our Vision

To become a Christ-centered community where everyone experiences faith, hope, and belonging.

What We Believe

We believe in God, Jesus Christ, the Holy Spirit, the Bible, prayer, grace, and eternal hope.
